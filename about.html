Welcome to Zannie's Rice Store

We believe good rice should be accessible to everyone. We source the finest varieties from trusted farms and offer them at fair, affordable prices.

Our Story

Founded in August 2024, Zannie's Rice Store began as a small family venture driven by our love for quality rice and serving our community. What started modestly has grown into a trusted local supplier for families and businesses alike.

From day one, quality and customer care have been at the heart of everything we do.

Our Values

  • Quality — Every grain meets high standards before it reaches you.
  • Sustainability — We partner with responsible farms that care about the land.
  • Customer First — Your satisfaction is our top priority with every order.

Meet the Team

We are a close-knit team of four family members. We handle everything together — selecting rice, packing orders, customer support, and delivery — so you always get personal, careful service.

Why Choose Us?

  • Fresh, high-quality rice varieties
  • Affordable prices + bulk discounts
  • Dedicated local customer support
  • Honest, transparent service
